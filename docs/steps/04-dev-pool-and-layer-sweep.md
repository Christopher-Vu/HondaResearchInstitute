# Step 4 — Dev pool and the layer sweep

**Owner:** both. **Depends on:** Step 3 (replay on Savio in bfloat16), B7 frozen
(`docs/steps/b7-gap-signal.md`, done 2026-10-09). **Blocks:** Steps 5–7, which
log only the layers and sites chosen here.

*Done when* (`PRD.md` §13): 2–3 layers and sites are chosen and written into the
config, with the sweep results committed.

## The dev pool, first wave: 190 rollouts

Config `configs/rollout/step4-dev-pool.yaml`; routes and manifest generated by
`adapters/simlingo/dev_routes.py` from the 220 Bench2Drive routes, seed 0, and
committed beside the config. Traffic-manager seed 1, so no rollout repeats a
Step 2 run. InterurbanActorFlow and InterurbanAdvancedActorFlow routes are left
out: Bench2Drive skipped the scenario on 7 of their 10 routes in Step 2, and the
runner rejects a skipped scenario as an invalid run.

Each dev route has a single weather entry. With two, the leaderboard re-sets the
weather as the car advances (`RouteWeatherBehavior`), interpolating 13
parameters that do not include `dust_storm`, so a dust storm would vanish within
the first few steps.

| Role | Rollouts | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Background | 100 | Base routes under a stock weather preset drawn from the presets SimLingo's data collection used, less `Default` (every field -1) and the dust storm: 20 presets. Includes P1's 30 base routes, whose background runs are P1's neighbourhood |
| P1 slice | 30 | One route from each of 30 scenario families, in a dust storm at night |
| P2 slice | 30 | The 5 DynamicObjectCrossing routes × distance {30, 40, 50} m × crossing angle {0, 10}°, container blocker, clear night |
| P2 neighbourhood | 30 | The same 30 combinations at clear noon |

**Why 190 and not 600–1,000.** `PRD.md` §13 says "a couple hundred" and the
§17.3 table says 600–1,000; they were written at different times. The sweep
compares 49 layer-site pairs on three kinds of metric, and a couple hundred
episodes is enough to rank them. A second wave runs only if the ranking is not
stable (below). At Step 2's measured 4.1 SU per route this wave costs about
**780 SU** and, at Step 2's pace of about 40 routes an hour on up to six A40s,
about **5 hours**.

## The practice gaps

`gaps/practice/practice_v1.yaml`, public and therefore excluded from the sealed
pool. The dev pool needs two so methods can be developed against a known answer
(`PRD.md` §9.2). Choosing them from Step 2's measured results ruled out the
obvious candidates:

- **Stock weather does not hurt SimLingo.** On Bench2Drive-220, night routes
  failed 24% and fog or heavy rain 31%, against 29% in clear daylight
  (measured, `results/savio/step2-bench2drive220/routes.jsonl` joined with the
  routes file's weather). A weather-preset gap would fail its efficacy check.
- **Every stock preset is in the training data.** SimLingo's data collection
  draws one of 22 whole CARLA presets per route, the first 22 in `dir(carla.WeatherParameters)` (`team_code/data_agent.py`,
  `shuffle_weather`), including the daytime dust storm.

So:

- **P1, dust storm at night (perceptual, control tier).** Dust storm parameters
  with the sun below the horizon. No collection preset combines them, so this is
  a Mode B region (§9.1). Neighbourhood: the same 30 routes under background
  weather.
- **P2, a pedestrian stepping out from behind a container at night
  (interactional).** DynamicObjectCrossing failed 0 of 5 in daylight in Step 2,
  and night alone does not raise failures, so a failure here belongs to the
  conjunction. Neighbourhood: the identical scenario grid at noon. P2 is not
  claimed to be absent from the training data; see `CONFLICTS.md` C21.

**Efficacy is measured, not assumed.** Each practice gap is checked against its
neighbourhood with the §9.1 thresholds (uplift ≥ 20 points, ratio ≥ 2, Fisher
p < 0.01, 30 in the slice). A gap that fails is reported, counted, and replaced
by a second candidate at about 250 SU for 60 rollouts. The practice-gap recovery
metric is computed only for gaps that pass.

## What each rollout records

`adapters/simlingo/capture_agent.py`, every policy step at 20 Hz:

- each of the 24 decoder layers' output, mean-pooled over all tokens and over the
  30 driving queries (two sites);
- the vision bridge (the projector between the image encoder and the language
  model) mean-pooled over image tokens (third site, one layer);
- the pre-registered state list of `PRD.md` §9.2: ego speed, lead distance and
  relative speed, nearest-pedestrian distance, traffic-light state, distance
  and time to the next junction, and an occlusion flag from a ray cast to the
  nearest actor ahead;
- collision events with their step, so failures have a time;
- the exact model input every 10th step, for replay.

## The sweep

`adapters/simlingo/export_activations.py` turns each rollout's capture into a
compact shard; `src/discovery/layer_sweep.py` runs on the shards with no GPU.
For each site and layer:

| Metric | Data | Model |
|---|---|---|
| Failure AUROC | Episode-mean activation, every rollout | Logistic regression, out-of-fold |
| State probes | Frames from successful rollouts only (§9.2) | Ridge R² for continuous states, AUROC for red light and occlusion |
| Practice-gap recovery | Episode means of failing rollouts | k-means for k = 2–8; best Jaccard with the gap's failing rollouts |

Cross-validation groups by base route, so variants of one route never sit on
both sides of a split.

**Selection rule, fixed before the sweep runs.** Rank every layer-site pair on
each metric family (state metrics averaged into one family, gaps likewise), and
average the ranks. Choose the best two pairs, then add the best pair for
practice-gap recovery if it is not already chosen: 2–3 choices. A second wave
of about 200 background rollouts runs only if dropping a random fifth of the
base routes changes the chosen set in more than one of five resamples.

## Order of work

1. Step 3 on Savio: replay saved Step 2 frames in CUDA bfloat16 against their
   logs (`replay_capture.py`, one short job).
2. A local smoke route on the Mac with the new capture fields, and the dust-at-night
   camera check, since the agent rejects a uniform camera frame.
3. Generate and submit the dev pool:
   `bash scripts/submit.sh gpu_a40 --time=0-03:00:00 --array=0-94%6 --exclude=n0214.savio3,n0215.savio3 scripts/route_sample.sbatch configs/rollout/step4-dev-pool.yaml 2`
4. Score the practice gaps' efficacy, export, sweep, write the chosen layers into
   the config.
