\begin{abstract}
A driving team that sees its policy fail wants to know which failures belong
together, so it can collect data for each group. We ask whether the policy's own
internal activations, read without any scenario label, sort its failures into the
groups a benchmark already knows. We use Fail2Drive's paired routes, develop
methods on SimLingo, and score frozen methods on TransFuser++ and UniAD against
rivals built only from what a vehicle fleet logs. This draft describes the data,
the methods, the scoring rules and their pre-registration history, and what went
wrong on the way. It reports no held-out outcome.
\end{abstract}
