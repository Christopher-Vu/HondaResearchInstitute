\paragraph{The question.}
The views pre-registration (\S\ref{sec:prereg-views}) asks which way of looking
at a driving model finds its Fail2Drive gaps best, and whether any way ranks
above the car's behaviour, which a fleet already logs. A row ranks above the
fleet if its 95\% interval against B3-behaviour lies above 0; otherwise it ties
or ranks below. UniAD is the only model whose outcomes no row had seen, so the
UniAD-alone columns are the blind test. The pooled columns add SimLingo and
TransFuser++, whose outcomes had already been read. This section reports the
final UniAD read, which the pre-registration names as the result of record. The
interim read is reported only where it explains a change.

\paragraph{Coverage.}
% measured: 1,198 of 1,200 routes, 598 of 600 twin pairs, 837 failing, all with frames; job 41710304 (comparison-table.md, Final UniAD read, Coverage)
The final read covers 1,198 of UniAD's 1,200 test routes and 598 of its 600
twin pairs (job 41710304). Two routes, 10301059 and 10501067, have no valid
attempt. Of the 1,198 routes, 837 fail, and every failing route has camera
frames.
% measured: 837 of 837 failing routes described, 6 grouping runs, run 1 resent once, 28 of 59 mode names flagged (comparison-table.md, Final UniAD read, caption job 41948480)
The caption row described all 837 failing routes (job 41948480) and ran its six
grouping runs; run 1 returned no structured output and was sent again once, as
in the interim read. The synonym list flagged 28 of the 59 mode names, which
means those names match Fail2Drive's type words.
% measured: SimLingo 1,185 routes, 357 failing, 7 units; TF++ 1,071 routes, 230 failing, 6 units (comparison-table.md, Primary units table, Coverage and Units rows)
% measured: 172 of 257 re-driven pairs reproduced, route match 423/524 (comparison-table.md, Primary units table, footnote 3)
SimLingo's and TransFuser++'s outcomes do not change between the reads. In the
interim table SimLingo's caption column covers 1,185 routes, 357 failing, over 7
primary units. TransFuser++'s frames come from a re-drive, so its caption routes
are only the pairs whose failure reproduced, 172 of 257 (\S\ref{sec:whatbroke}),
with 1,071 routes and 230 failing over 6 primary units. The pooled caption row
is scored on those re-driven routes. % source: docs/research/experiments/f2d-unpooled/uniad_read.sh step 4 (TR = f2d-test-tfpp/routes.jsonl); comparison-table.md, Final UniAD read, caption job paragraph
The pooled activation columns of
Table~\ref{tab:final} use TransFuser++'s own test-pool drive, so the activation
rows in the caption files differ slightly from those columns. The table does not
state that drive's coverage.

\paragraph{The units the rule admitted.}
% design: 1.5x and at least 5 failing routes (docs/prereg-views.md, Units, metric and intervals)
The unit rule (\S\ref{sec:fail2drive}) runs inside each read, on that read's
routes.
% measured: PedestriansOnRoad 16/30 vs 0/30, RightConstruction 11/30 vs 6/30, Wall admitted; FullyBlocked 30/30 vs 21/30, ratio 1.43 (comparison-table.md, Final UniAD read)
On the final read it admits three UniAD types. PedestriansOnRoad fails on 16 of
30 generalisation routes and on none of 30 base twins. RightConstruction fails
on 11 of 30 against 6 of 30. Wall also passes the rule. FullyBlocked drops out:
it fails on all 30 generalisation routes but on 21 of 30 base twins, a ratio of
1.43, below the rule's 1.5. Without Wall, UniAD therefore has two primary units,
PedestriansOnRoad and RightConstruction. The named-gap class, from the gap rule
of \S\ref{sec:fail2drive}, is PedestriansOnRoad and Wall. The interim read had
admitted FullyBlocked as a third primary unit and had an empty named class.

\paragraph{No activation view ranks above behaviour on the blind model.}
% measured: query-mean +0.049 [-0.006, 0.138], img-mean +0.040 [-0.019, 0.160] (comparison-table.md, Final UniAD read, rows query-mean and img-mean, UniAD primary)
On UniAD's primary units the two new activation views come closest, with
\code{query-mean} at $+0.049$ $[-0.006, 0.138]$ and \code{img-mean} at $+0.040$
$[-0.019, 0.160]$. Both intervals include 0, so both tie behaviour.
% measured: best new view without caption re-selected per draw, query-mean in 59%, +0.049 [-0.003, 0.163]; vs m23 +0.058 [0.008, 0.173] (comparison-table.md, Final UniAD read, Reading, third item)
When the best activation view is chosen again in every draw, it is
\code{query-mean} in 59\% of draws, and it still ties: $+0.049$ $[-0.003,
0.163]$. That choice does rank above the frozen finalist m23, at $+0.058$
$[0.008, 0.173]$.
% measured: m23 -0.009 [-0.025, -0.002] on UniAD primary (comparison-table.md, Final UniAD read, row m23)
The finalists tie behaviour or fall below it. On UniAD's primary units m23's
interval, $[-0.025, -0.002]$, lies below 0.
% measured: m23 +0.049 [0.017, 0.080], m40 +0.048 [0.009, 0.083] pooled all rule (comparison-table.md, Final UniAD read, rows m23 and m40)
The only activation cells above behaviour are m23 and m40 on the pooled
all-rule units, at $+0.049$ and $+0.048$. Those units include Wall, and m23 and
m40 led only with Wall in on dev and on TransFuser++ as well. B7-pool is below behaviour or ties it in every
column.

\paragraph{The caption row and B3-infraction rank above behaviour.}
% measured: caption UniAD primary +0.048 [0.010, 0.114], all rule +0.059, named +0.083; pooled primary +0.065 [0.030, 0.095] (comparison-table.md, Final UniAD read, row caption)
The caption row's interval lies above 0 in every column. On UniAD's primary
units it is $+0.048$ $[0.010, 0.114]$; on all rule units $+0.059$, and on the
named gaps $+0.083$. Pooled over the three models, on the primary units, it is
$+0.065$ $[0.030, 0.095]$.
% measured: best new view incl. caption re-selected per draw, UniAD primary caption 51%, img-mean 31%, query-mean 17%, +0.049 [0.014, 0.165]; pooled primary caption 98%, +0.065 [0.032, 0.095] (comparison-table.md, Final UniAD read, Reading, second item)
When the best of all three new views is chosen again in every draw, the choice
still ranks above behaviour: on UniAD's primary units it is the caption row in
51\% of draws, \code{img-mean} in 31\% and \code{query-mean} in 17\%, at
$+0.049$ $[0.014, 0.165]$. Pooled, it is the caption row in 98\% of draws, at
$+0.065$ $[0.032, 0.095]$.
% measured: caption minus query-mean +0.001 [-0.082, 0.082] on UniAD; query-mean minus caption -0.055 [-0.096, -0.003] pooled primary (comparison-table.md, Final UniAD read, Reading, first item)
Against the other rows directly the caption row is not ahead on UniAD alone. It
ties \code{query-mean} there, at $+0.001$ $[-0.082, 0.082]$, and it ties
\code{img-mean}. Pooled on the primary units it ranks above every other row; for
\code{query-mean} the paired difference is $-0.055$ $[-0.096, -0.003]$ the
other way.
% measured: caption per unit on UniAD 0.11 / 0.03 / 0.15 (Ped / RightConstr / Wall) (comparison-table.md, Final UniAD read, Reading, first item)
Per unit on UniAD, the caption row scores 0.11 on PedestriansOnRoad, 0.03 on
RightConstruction and 0.15 on Wall.
% measured: B3-infraction UniAD +0.015 [0.002, 0.042], +0.018 [0.004, 0.041], +0.024 [0.004, 0.056] (comparison-table.md, Final UniAD read, row B3-infraction)
B3-infraction, which groups failures by the infraction log, is the only other
row above behaviour in every UniAD column, by small margins: $+0.015$ $[0.002,
0.042]$ on the primary units. It is a reported rival with no pass bar
(\S\ref{sec:rivals}).
The caption row uses an external language model, Claude Opus 5.5, on camera
frames. It is a strong baseline that describes what is in view, not a reading
of the driving model's internals. So the rows that rank above behaviour on the
blind model are a description of the camera image and the infraction log, and
no row that reads the model's activations.

\paragraph{The interim lead of \code{query-mean} did not hold.}
% measured: interim UniAD alone primary query-mean 0.208, +0.169 [0.047, 0.206]; 869 routes, 552 failing, 3 units (comparison-table.md, Primary units table, column "UniAD alone (blind, interim)")
On the interim read, 869 routes of which 552 failed, \code{query-mean} scored
0.208 on UniAD's three primary units, $+0.169$ $[0.047, 0.206]$ above
behaviour.
% measured: interim per unit query-mean FullyBlocked 0.08, PedestriansOnRoad 0.42, RightConstruction 0.13; one axis exactly PedestriansOnRoad's 14 failing routes (comparison-table.md, "Where the leads come from")
The per-unit rows show where that lead came from. \code{query-mean} scored 0.42
on PedestriansOnRoad against 0.08 and 0.13 on the other two units, and one of
its clusters held exactly the 14 failing PedestriansOnRoad routes driven by
then. The interim table flagged that one unit carried the lead.
% measured: final query-mean PedestriansOnRoad 0.07 with all 16 failing routes among 837 failures; per unit query-mean 0.07 / 0.09 / 0.05, img-mean 0.03 / 0.11 / 0.05, B3-behaviour 0.04 / 0.02 / 0.07 (Ped / RightConstr / Wall) (comparison-table.md, Final UniAD read, Reading, fourth item)
On the final read, with all 16 failing PedestriansOnRoad routes among 837
failures, \code{query-mean} scores 0.07 on that unit. Its per-unit scores are
0.07, 0.09 and 0.05 on PedestriansOnRoad, RightConstruction and Wall, against
B3-behaviour's 0.04, 0.02 and 0.07. On the primary units its difference from
behaviour is now $+0.049$, and the interval includes 0. m03 is fit on the pool
it scores (\S\ref{sec:m03}), so the final read fits it on 1,198 routes instead
of 869. The table records that the cluster did not form again, but not why. The
pre-registration fixed before either read that the final read, not the interim,
is the result of record (\S\ref{sec:prereg-views}).

\paragraph{What the table does not show.}
% measured: 219 tries at the views tag (docs/prereg-views.md; comparison-table.md, Caveats)
The three new views are 3 of 219 tries counted at the views tag. Selection
among them is paid for by choosing again in every draw. Only UniAD is blind;
SimLingo's and TransFuser++'s outcomes were read before these rows were scored.
UniAD's primary score is a mean over two units, so intervals are wide and one
unit can move it a long way, as the interim lead showed. Recovery of known
Fail2Drive types stands in for discovery of unknown failure groups
(\S\ref{sec:limitations}).
