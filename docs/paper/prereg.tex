\section{Pre-registration history}
\label{sec:prereg}

The study changed its rules several times. Each change was written down, dated
and tagged in git before the data it governs was read, and each change made
after some data had been seen is labelled \emph{post hoc}. This section lists
them in order, because a reader can only weigh a result against the rules that
were fixed before it. The texts are \code{docs/prereg-fail2drive.md} (the base
and amendments A1--A4) and \code{docs/prereg-views.md}; the reasoning for each is
in \code{CONFLICTS.md} entries C38--C43.

\subsection{Base pre-registration}

Approved by Chris and tagged \code{f2d-prereg-v1} on 2026-11-06, before either
model's full screen or test pool was read. It fixes the test pools (every pair,
seeds 101--106), the gap, ceiling, decoy and background classification
(\S\ref{sec:fail2drive}), the B4-unnamed split, label hiding, the synonym list for
the contamination check, and a primary test of the best activation method against
the best non-oracle baseline with a fixed margin. Sections A1--A3 replace most of
its analysis plan; its pools, gap rule and label hiding stand.

\subsection{A1: the discovery sprint (post hoc, 2026-11-14)}

Written after two methods (v1 and v2) had been scored on SimLingo's test pool,
and before any sprint method. TransFuser++'s shards were still unread.
\begin{itemize}
  \item B3-behaviour replaces full B3, which read simulator distances.
  \item Every method and rival ranks its own axes; only the first 10 count. The
        old rule took each method's best axis using the key, which flatters
        every method and most of all those that emit many axes.
  \item The fixed pass/fail margin is dropped. Every comparison reports the
        difference, a 95\% interval and a one-sided $p$ from the pair bootstrap,
        against B3-behaviour (primary) and against the best rival re-taken in
        every replicate (secondary).
  \item At most three finalists, one primary, scored once on each held-out
        model; the headline pools TransFuser++ and the third model, with
        per-model and per-gap results always beside it.
  \item Caption methods are dropped, because no test pool was thought to have
        frames. That was later found to be wrong for SimLingo
        (\S\ref{sec:whatbroke}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{A2: distinct axes and the infraction rival (post hoc, 2026-11-16)}

Written after 134 dev configurations had been scored.
% measured: 134 configurations at A2 (docs/prereg-fail2drive.md §8)
\begin{itemize}
  \item A method's top 10 counts distinct axes only. Under A1, Ward cuts 2, 3
        and 5 listed a cluster again whenever a later cut left it whole, so most
        methods competed with 7 or 8 distinct axes, and how many depended on the
        shape of the tree.
  \item B3-infraction is reported beside every method, with no pass bar and
        outside the best-rival comparison, because it was proposed after the
        leads were seen.
  \item Every logged method is rescored under A2 on both dev pools. A rescore is
        not a try.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{A3: the freeze (2026-11-17)}
\label{sec:prereg-a3}

Written after the SimLingo search ended and before any method read TransFuser++'s
or UniAD's test-pool data.
% measured: 188 tries at the freeze (docs/prereg-fail2drive.md §9)
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Finalists:} m23 (primary) and m40 (\S\ref{sec:mfamily}), chosen
        by Chris on the full dev record: the dev score, the H21 replication as
        context, stability under resampling, and readiness to run elsewhere.
  \item \textbf{Rival fix (post hoc):} B3-behaviour's approach window had been
        taken from a speed array capped at the first 100 frames
        (\S\ref{sec:whatbroke}). The cap was removed before any held-out score;
        the rival's features and grouping are unchanged.
  \item \textbf{Transfer spec} made binding, including the B7 sparsity rule,
        which was fixed blind to every held-out score.
  \item \textbf{Order:} TransFuser++ first, alone, labelled interim; then UniAD;
        then the pooled headline, with its two-model scorer written and tested
        before any UniAD outcome is read.
  \item \textbf{Round 2, fixed in advance:} if m23's interval against
        B3-behaviour on TransFuser++ includes zero, TransFuser++ becomes a
        second dev pool, the search may continue on both models, at most three
        new finalists are frozen under a new tag, and they are confirmed on UniAD
        alone. Round 1 is reported in full whatever round 2 finds.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{A4: round 2 closed (post hoc, 2026-11-18)}

Round 2 was opened under A3's rule. After 12 round-2 tries, Chris stopped the
search: no round-2 candidate beat m23 on the pooled dev score, so nothing new was
frozen.
% measured: 12 round-2 tries, 202 total at the stop (docs/prereg-fail2drive.md §10)
UniAD therefore scores round 1's m23 and m40 under A3's rules. UniAD's outcomes
are read only after the views pre-registration is tagged, so that one read
serves both. This is post hoc with respect to round 2's dev scores and blind to
every UniAD outcome.

\subsection{The views pre-registration (\code{views-prereg-v1}, 2026-11-19)}
\label{sec:prereg-views}

Approved by Chris and tagged at commit \code{69668f5}, before any UniAD outcome
was read. It asks which ways of looking at a driving model find its gaps best,
and whether any beats the car's behaviour, as a ranking across all three models.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Headlines:} the pooled three-model ranking, labelled ``SimLingo
        and TransFuser++ outcomes already read''; and UniAD alone, labelled as the
        only blind test.
  \item \textbf{Units:} the rule units without Wall are primary
        (\S\ref{sec:fail2drive}); all rule units and the named gaps are reported
        beside them.
  \item \textbf{Rows:} the rivals and frozen finalists (not new tries), plus
        three new views, each a try: \code{img-mean} and \code{query-mean} (the
        approach-window mean over an image-token site and a query site, through
        m03) and \code{caption} (caption-then-cluster on camera frames).
        % measured: try count 216 to 219 (docs/prereg-views.md, Rows)
        The total goes from 216 to 219. A fourth row, \code{vit-mean}, is
        SimLingo-only, labelled post hoc, already counted, and in no ranking claim.
  % design: 2,000 draws (docs/prereg-views.md, Units, metric and intervals)
  \item \textbf{Metric and intervals:} rank-averaged best match primary, top-10
        best match secondary; pair bootstrap with 2{,}000 draws.
  \item \textbf{Decision rule:} a row ranks above the fleet if its interval
        against B3-behaviour lies above 0. Row A ranks above row B only if the
        paired interval of A minus B lies above 0; otherwise they tie, and the
        ranking is the partial order the intervals give. ``Behaviour ties or
        beats every view'' is named as an expected, reportable outcome.
  \item \textbf{UniAD reads (C43):} an interim read on 2026-11-21 at 09:00 PT
        on every route driven by then, labelled interim with its coverage; and
        the final read on the full pool, which is the result of record. Nothing
        changes between them.
  % design: 4 frames, 6 grouping runs (docs/prereg-views.md, The caption row)
  \item \textbf{Caption row:} one describing call per failing route on its last
        four front frames, then six independent grouping runs per model; the
        score is the median of the six inside every bootstrap draw. Frame
        folders are named by a new salt and sorted by hash. Prompts were run once
        on blank frames first, and every mode name is matched against the frozen
        synonym list and flagged.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Recorded after the tag, before any UniAD outcome.}
Two things were decided after \code{views-prereg-v1} and written into the same
file before UniAD was read. The tag fixed sites but not layers for TransFuser++
and UniAD; the last layer is used at every such site. And captioning moved from
agent sessions to a batch API with the same model and the same prompt text, with
each request's frames listed by hash as the audit record. SimLingo was captioned
again in full that way so each pool has one captioner.
