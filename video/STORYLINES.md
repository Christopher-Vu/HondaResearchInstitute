# Storylines

Recommendation: **A**, because one real road the viewer can see crash is the thing a stranger follows; B and C
are kept here in case a later turn switches.

## A. One road, three drives (picked)

- Device: the worked example. Form: one frame that rebuilds. The same Town04 highway frame (base route 26406)
  stays the home stage; the film keeps returning to it as each fault is planted, hidden, and found.
- The viewer sees the same drive three times, two of them crash, and then watches a tool that never saw the
  answer pull the black-patch drives out of a pile of 738, miss the frozen ones, and then find them through a
  clue in the model's own numbers.
- First two seconds: three copies of the same real camera frame side by side; the middle one gets a black
  rectangle, the right one stops moving while the left keeps driving.
- Beats: hook (three drives) → the planted causes → the question → the haystack of 738 → the bar (one in five)
  → inside the model (896 numbers) → the dictionary → contrastive diffing → payoff: P4 found → the frozen
  camera is invisible → the clue (numbers repeat) → second method finds P7 → the delay still hides → the honest
  part (re-running on fresh drives) → claim → end card.
- Look: concrete-grey ground with 3% grain, near-black ink, safety orange only on what the tool finds; the real
  CARLA frames are the only photographs.
- Sound: narrated; the voice is the clock. No music source is connected, so no bed (stated in the delivery note).

## B. The evidence wall

Device: a detective's board. Form: one continuous take across a pinboard where each crashed drive is a photo
pinned up and string groups them. Bolder, but the board metaphor competes with the real frames.

## C. Into the numbers

Device: the object is the explanation. Form: an endless zoom from the windscreen into the model's 896 numbers,
into one dictionary pattern, back out to the road. Striking, but abstract for a viewer who has not seen a neural
network.
