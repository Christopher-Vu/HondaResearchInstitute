## How the audit was done

1. **Topic-based verification agents** (11 groups) checked each claim against primary sources where they could reach them: GitHub repos, READMEs and configs; project pages; the official BibTeX; HF paper pages; conference sites. Otherwise they used strong secondary sources: search-engine snippets of arXiv/OpenReview pages, GitHub-mirrored arXiv digests, and full-text paper mirrors.
2. **Adversarial second pass.** Every negative, partial or high-stakes verdict (41 of them) went to an independent agent told to *overturn* it. Where the second pass disagreed, **its verdict is the one shown**, with the first-pass verdict noted.
3. **Orchestrator spot checks** settled single-source or conflicting items. See the table below.

**Access limits (important for interpreting "unverifiable").** The sandbox's egress proxy **blocked arxiv.org, alphaxiv, OpenReview, Hugging Face (for some agents), the CVF site, neurips.cc, aaai.org and several publisher sites**. The web-search budget was also used up partway through. Numbers that appear only inside a paper PDF are therefore marked [partial] or [unverifiable] unless a second source confirmed them. Before submission, **P5 re-checks every [partial] / [unverifiable] item that ends up in the paper against the PDF.**

**Legend.** [confirmed] · [corrected] confirmed, with corrections to the source text · [partial] partially confirmed · [unverifiable] · [contradicted] · [fabricated] likely fabricated.

## Headline findings (what changed because of this audit)

**Mentor, compute and simulator**
1. **Mentor affiliation.** Ryan Lingo is an Applied AI Research Engineer & Developer Advocate at **99P Labs / Honda Research Institute USA**. "Toyota Research Institute" in v1 is [contradicted].
2. **Compute.** There is **no LBNL cluster called "Salvo"** ([fabricated]); it is probably UC Berkeley's **Savio**. The NRP Nautilus endpoint exists, but its model roster has changed (now vision-capable `qwen3` / `gemma` etc.).
3. **Simulator.** **Drake** is not a driving simulator ([contradicted]); use CARLA 0.9.15 + Bench2Drive.

**Policies**
4. **DriveVLM has no public weights or code** ([contradicted] "open-weight").
5. OpenVLA and π₀ have no official driving checkpoints. The only public π₀-style driving policy found is Drive-π0 in the DriveMoE repo (initialised from PaliGemma-3B, not from π₀ weights).
6. Of the policies named in the v1 prompt (DriveVLM, OpenVLA/π₀ variants, SimLingo), **SimLingo** is the only open-weight driving VLA with a CARLA closed-loop agent, and it also ships training data and code. Other open CARLA-capable driving VLAs exist, e.g. ORION, Drive-π0/DriveMoE and LMDrive (CARLA 0.9.10.1); see D3.

**Venues**
7. **AAAI-27 (Jul 28), ICRA 2027 (Sep 16) and ICLR 2027 (Sep 25) have passed.**
